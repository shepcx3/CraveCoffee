01 — Our story

Downtown Kingston’s living room.

CRAVE is where the city slows down for a minute. Pull up a stool at the bar, sink into the leather banquettes, or grab a table on the patio and watch Princess Street go by.

Every cup is pulled with care — from a silky flat white with latte art to our iced lattes crowned with cold foam — and the bakery case is stocked fresh each morning. Come for the coffee. Stay because you’ll want to.
